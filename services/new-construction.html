New Construction Inspections

Because “new” doesn’t always mean problem-free

Why Inspect a New Home?

New construction homes can still have issues ranging from improper installations to missed details during fast-paced builds. A professional inspection helps ensure your home meets expected quality and safety standards before you close.

What We Look For

Structural & framing concerns
Roofing and exterior details
HVAC installation & operation
Electrical & plumbing workmanship
Interior finishes & safety issues

Builder-Neutral, Buyer-Focused

Our inspections are conducted professionally and objectively. We focus on factual findings and clear documentation to help buyers address concerns with confidence, without unnecessary confrontation.
