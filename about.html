Meet Scott Thomas

Hi, I’m Scott Thomas, owner and lead inspector at All American Inspections. I’ve spent over 25 years performing home inspections, and I approach every property as if it were my own.

I’m a hard-working family man who takes pride in providing honest, thorough, and professional inspections. I’m South Carolina licensed and certified through both ICA and InterNACHI, and I stay current with industry standards and best practices.

My goal is simple: give you clear, easy-to-understand information so you can make confident decisions about one of the biggest investments of your life.
